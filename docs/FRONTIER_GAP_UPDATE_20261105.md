# Frontier gap update — 2026-11-05

**Purpose:** update the paper's claims after the completed registered 40k B study. This update launches no experiment and authorizes no policy loading, sealed-test access, Attention/GNN/L1, complete paper-suite rerun, or additional training.

## What changed in the positioning

Three close lines of prior work constrain the narrative. [SCOPE (IJCAI 2026)](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2026/7) already joins learned future previews, cooperative encirclement, and execution-time correction. [CC-HGCBF (October 2026 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41214) represents state-dependent motion capability for heterogeneous teams. [CPA risk-guided flight (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23565) learns a directional anticipation signal for visual navigation. The reviewed [2026 multi-agent racing work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22748) also means agile flight must include multi-agent interactions in our discussion.

The defensible question is therefore: **under a specified capture contract, training budget, and representation capacity, which probe content and capture-oriented training interventions improve joint completion, and which fail?** This is a falsifiable experimental question. A fixed bank of geometric rollouts may be useful without being a new safety formalism.

SCOPE's [official IJCAI 2026 record](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2026/7) and [CC-HGCBF's preprint/method](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.41214v1) were rechecked after the 40k result on 2026-11-05. SCOPE supplies learned previews plus runtime enforcement; CC-HGCBF constructs an approximate finite-horizon admissible-velocity representation and gives a conditional continuous-time safety result. Neither source turns B's fixed shaping identity into a new theorem. The completed static B comparison does not test dynamic probe information.

## Completed B study and the next evidence gap

The [episode-level Windows analysis](../results/masac_v4_windows_audit_20261105/analysis.json) covers the three probe variants, three training seeds, and 12 development maps per checkpoint. It distinguishes **any-agent entry** (there exist an agent and a time at which that agent enters its ball) from **all-agent eventual entry** (each agent enters at some time, possibly different times). Joint capture additionally requires the implemented simultaneous position/speed predicate. High any-agent entry rates cannot establish all-agent eventual entry. In this audit, 61/65 failures lack all-agent eventual entry; only 4/65 reach that milestone and still fail joint capture. Therefore, the earlier interpretation that individual arrival is largely solved and synchronization is the principal cause is unsupported.

B currently implements **fixed** shaping from a smooth worst-agent distance/speed potential, with no curriculum schedule or explicit arrival-time-gap penalty. Its benefits and causal mechanism remain unproven. The method name does not imply that timing alone has been isolated as the bottleneck. Preserve separate arrival-coverage, individual capture and simultaneous-capture diagnostics so the experiment can reveal which symptom, if any, changes.

The [40k protocol](design/MASAC_V4_SYNC_CAPTURE_40K_PROTOCOL_20261105.md) was pushed before launch. All six runs are now **COMPLETE**. The registered engineering rule returns **`ADVANCEMENT_RULE_MET`**, which supports **planning confirmation**. It does not authorize continuation or a new launch. Both arms remain below the absolute 8/12 development gate on every seed: **FAIL 0/3 each**. The positive mean coexists with a regressing seed and low absolute completion. It is not evidence of statistical significance, broad generalization, or predictive-channel usefulness. Source/configuration, final endpoints and decision rules remain frozen; intermediate peaks and sealed-map outcomes cannot replace them.

Across final-40k trajectories, **0/72 collide**; **43 of 54 failures lack all-agent eventual ball entry**, **1 reaches all balls without all-agent eventual individual capture**, and **10 attain individual captures at some times without joint capture**. The three seed summaries are [1511](../results/masac_v4_sync_40k_progress_20261105/trajectory_40000_seed1511/summary.json), [1522](../results/masac_v4_sync_40k_progress_20261105/trajectory_40000_seed1522/summary.json), and [1533](../results/masac_v4_sync_40k_progress_20261105/trajectory_40000_seed1533/summary.json). Zero observed collisions supplies no forward-invariance guarantee. These final diagnostics replace neither the historical 108-episode counts nor their different failure population.

The preceding two-seed 2k paired pilot completed finite updates but all four final evaluations were 0/12. This validated execution, not capture efficacy. The 40k study used cold starts, not continuation from missing Mac checkpoints. Its training-map generator is paired by episode index, while policy-dependent episode lengths can change map exposure at a fixed transition count. Describe that limitation explicitly.

The [next minimal plan](design/MASAC_V4_POST40K_NEXT_MINIMAL_PLAN_20261105.md) is restricted to **offline feature recomputation**: for each of the six final replays, uniformly select 1,024 entries from its last 8,000 chronological transitions, freeze indices and input hashes, and compute full/static/static-constant-fill-1 on identical observations. It requires object-validity provenance, clipping/sentinel separation, all 11 channel differences, and traceable TTC/reserve/flag propagation. It loads no policy, generates no action, runs no training and opens no sealed test. Its population is late-training static control/B replay, not the old full-policy or final-policy distribution.

Useful hypotheses remain **unproven**: which state-dependent peer features are available and useful, whether their CV assumptions hold in close interactions, and which mechanism changes team-wide approach versus capture retention. The current B result does not identify why historical full is weaker. The pure PBRS identity preserves the stated exact Path-C soft objective under its boundary assumptions; it does not turn Path-C into a sparse-success objective or guarantee faster SAC learning. Additional training is a separately designed confirmation proposal, not an automatic extension of the completed 40k budget.
