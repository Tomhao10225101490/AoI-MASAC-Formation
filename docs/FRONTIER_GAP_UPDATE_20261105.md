# Frontier gap update — 2026-11-05

**Purpose:** narrow the paper's claims against primary literature while the registered B study runs. This update launches no experiment and authorizes no Attention, L1, complete paper-suite rerun, or new external-baseline training.

## What changed in the positioning

Three close lines of prior work constrain the narrative. [SCOPE (IJCAI 2026)](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2026/7) already joins learned future previews, cooperative encirclement, and execution-time correction. [CC-HGCBF (October 2026 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41214) represents state-dependent motion capability for heterogeneous teams. [CPA risk-guided flight (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23565) learns a directional anticipation signal for visual navigation. The reviewed [2026 multi-agent racing work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22748) also means agile flight must include multi-agent interactions in our discussion.

The defensible question is therefore: **under a specified capture contract, training budget, and representation capacity, which probe content and capture-oriented training interventions improve joint completion, and which fail?** This is a falsifiable experimental question. A fixed bank of geometric rollouts may be useful without being a new safety formalism.

## B sync-capture: research controls required

The [episode-level Windows analysis](../results/masac_v4_windows_audit_20261105/analysis.json) covers the three probe variants, three training seeds, and 12 development maps per checkpoint. It distinguishes **any-agent entry** (there exist an agent and a time at which that agent enters its ball) from **all-agent eventual entry** (each agent enters at some time, possibly different times). Joint capture additionally requires the implemented simultaneous position/speed predicate. High any-agent entry rates cannot establish all-agent eventual entry. In this audit, 61/65 failures lack all-agent eventual entry; only 4/65 reach that milestone and still fail joint capture. Therefore, the earlier interpretation that individual arrival is largely solved and synchronization is the principal cause is unsupported.

B currently implements **fixed** shaping from a smooth worst-agent distance/speed potential, with no curriculum schedule or explicit arrival-time-gap penalty. Its benefits and causal mechanism remain unproven. The method name does not imply that timing alone has been isolated as the bottleneck. Preserve separate arrival-coverage, individual capture and simultaneous-capture diagnostics so the experiment can reveal which symptom, if any, changes.

The [40k protocol](design/MASAC_V4_SYNC_CAPTURE_40K_PROTOCOL_20261105.md) was pushed before launch. It fixes cold seeds1511/1522/1533, two static arms, final40k endpoints, eval2k, and the engineering advancement rule. The six-history study is incomplete; the final study decision remains pending. The first completed pair is recorded separately in [its progress note](SYNC_CAPTURE_FIRST_PAIR_20261105.md), without a treatment-effect claim. Source/configuration and decision rules remain frozen; no intermediate peak, sealed-map result or extra budget can replace the registered endpoint.

The [two-pair progress snapshot](SYNC_CAPTURE_TWO_PAIR_PROGRESS_20261105.md) now records1511 control/B=2/12 versus6/12 and1522=1/12 versus0/12;1533 is still running. The first positive paired difference does not recur on the second seed. In1522, higher any/all eventual entry and individual capture coverage for B coexist with lower joint success. This reinforces the gap between proxy coverage and the actual simultaneous predicate, without identifying its cause or supplying the pending three-seed decision. Neither this static B comparison nor standard PBRS theory identifies the utility of dynamic probe content.

The preceding two-seed2k paired pilot completed finite updates but all four final evaluations were0/12. This validated execution, not capture efficacy. The40k run is cold start, not continuation from missing Mac checkpoints. Its training-map generator is paired by episode index, while policy-dependent episode lengths can change map exposure at a fixed transition count. Describe that limitation explicitly.

Useful hypotheses remain **unproven**: worst-agent distance/speed guidance may improve team-wide arrival, and a separately designed curriculum may increase the frequency of near-success joint states. The existing ablation and terminal classifications alone cannot identify why full is weaker, establish a synchronization-specific cause, or predict whether B improves it. The pure PBRS identity preserves the stated exact Path-C soft objective under its boundary assumptions; it does not turn Path-C into a sparse-success objective or guarantee faster SAC learning.
